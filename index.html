Zap It

How to play

  1. Press Start to begin a 60-second round.
  2. Match the target item shown at the top.
  3. Zap the matching item by tapping or clicking it.
  4. Correct zaps add points and build your streak.
  5. Misses reset your streak and cost 10 seconds.

Desktop: use the numpad like the board: 7-8-9 across the top, 4-5-6 in the middle, and 1-2-3 along the bottom.

Press Space to restart the round.
